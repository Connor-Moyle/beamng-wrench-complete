# BeamNG repository post (draft)

Formatting is BBCode, as the BeamNG forum and repository use. Screenshots go where the `[IMG]` lines are.

## Title

Wrench Complete: a street racing career, a hands-on garage and drivers (West Coast USA)

## Short pitch

Start on foot with a few thousand dollars. Buy a wreck, build it in a real garage, race the streets at night and climb to the top of the ROC. Garage, career, drivers and weather effects in one mod.

## Post

```
[SIZE=5][B]Wrench Complete[/B][/SIZE]
A complete street racing career for West Coast USA, in one mod.

You start on foot with a few thousand dollars and no car. Buy a wreck, take it apart and build it back in a real garage, earn money by day, race the streets by night and work your way up to the top of the ROC. A driver sits in every car, rain makes the road wet, and your cars get dirty.

[IMG]docs/screenshots/01_rain_wet_paint.jpg[/IMG]
[IMG]docs/screenshots/10_driver_hands_cockpit.jpg[/IMG]

[SIZE=4][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]
[B]Garage[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Drive in and the car goes onto a working lift
[*]Click parts off and on, tune, paint and repair
[*]Order parts or buy them over the counter
[*]A radio that plays your own music
[/LIST]
[B]Career[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Day: towing, scrapyard runs, taxi fares, dealer deliveries, track events
[*]Night: street races, car meets, pink slips, the swap meet
[*]Dealers, the junkyard, online auctions and a live auction
[*]Rivals, tier bosses and a campaign end
[*]A phone (K): messages, map, camera, bank, jobs, contacts, cab and help
[/LIST]
[B]Drivers[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A driver in every car: hands on the wheel, feet on the pedals, leaning with the car
[*]Drivers are hurt in crashes and react to it
[/LIST]
[B]Weather and cars[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Wet, glossy roads in the rain, puddles and wheel spray
[*]Dirty paint, drops on the glass, snow, frost, bugs and scratches
[/LIST]
[B]Settings[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Everything in one place: ESC > Mods, with presets (Performance, Balanced, Quality, Max)
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Install[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Copy [I]wrench_complete.zip[/I] (do not unzip it) into your mods folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods
[*]Remove the separate Wrench Works, Street Legal, Drivers & Pedestrians, Driver Injuries and Car Surface FX mods if you have them
[*]Start the game: Career > Wrench Complete > New career > West Coast USA
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Requirements[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]BeamNG.drive 0.39
[*]The map West Coast USA (comes with the game)
[*]On a laptop or lower-spec PC, pick the Performance preset in ESC > Mods > Quick setup
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Known issues[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Plays on West Coast USA only
[*]Some features have had little play in the game yet: night race variety, auctions, rivals and the campaign end, the phone camera and gallery, the Look and cinematic camera graphics options. Please report anything odd
[*]Heavy rain with many puddles costs frames: lower [I]Puddles in the rain[/I] or use the Performance preset
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Credits[/B][/SIZE]
Made by Connor Moyle. A tribute to the feel of Street Legal Racing: Redline; none of that game's files are used.
```
